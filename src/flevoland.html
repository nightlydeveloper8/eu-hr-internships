Employer sites and collection status

How this research works

The municipal composition and 19 settlements come from CBS 2026 data. Sector organisations, public institutions and official employer sites support discovery. Official career pages and recruiting systems supply vacancies. Job location, HR tasks and internship status must be explicit; a local head office does not make an internship elsewhere local.

Collection is scheduled every morning at 06:17 UTC (07:17 in winter and 08:17 in summer locally). Each employer receives an automatic attempt. “Partial” means more pages or interpretation still need work; “blocked” means the site could not be read. These do not count as a completed check. General programmes retain the date of their research check.

This is an expanding research inventory. It does not yet establish every medium or large employer in the province.
