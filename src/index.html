About our sources

How we verify internships

Direct application verified

We confirmed the current vacancy and the employer’s own application route for it within the last 48 hours (policy v2).

Public employment service

Listed as active by an official public employment service (such as EURES) within 48 hours. Apply through the official advertisement; the employer’s own form was not checked.

Lead to check

Previously collected, but the current vacancy or application route could not be confirmed. Not a confirmed opening — and not confirmed closed.

Graduate traineeships are not listed; a trainee title counts only when the posting documents a student placement. Part-time student jobs (Werkstudent, working student) are not internships. Zero entries means no current internship in our collection, not that a country has none.

How we classify languages and internships

Only explicit internship or placement signals are accepted; a working-student title counts only if it also names an internship. HR specialisations use multilingual title rules. An English advertisement never proves workplace English. Confirmed workplace-language labels have a short supporting excerpt. They do not guarantee that English alone is sufficient. Advertisement language marked “estimated” uses a local heuristic.

Verified vacancies require a matching current vacancy and an available application form or reviewed application entry. A live URL alone is insufficient. “Source blocked” identifies access restrictions; “Verification incomplete” identifies missing evidence, stale checks or a recently missing listing. No eligibility or work-permit determination is made here.
