How we classify languages and internships

Only explicit internship, placement, working-student or traineeship signals are accepted. HR specialisations use multilingual title rules. An English advertisement never proves workplace English. Confirmed workplace-language labels have a short supporting excerpt. They do not guarantee that English alone is sufficient. Advertisement language marked “estimated” uses a local heuristic.

Verified vacancies require a matching current vacancy and an available application form or reviewed application entry. A live URL alone is insufficient. “Source blocked” identifies access restrictions; “Verification incomplete” identifies missing evidence, stale checks or a recently missing listing. Read the original posting for current requirements. No eligibility or work-permit determination is made here.

Bookmarks stay in this browser. No accounts, analytics or tracking cookies. Collection is scheduled daily. The timestamp shows the last published collection, and a warning appears after 36 hours. Check the original employer posting for current requirements.
